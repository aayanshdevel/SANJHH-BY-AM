Our Story

Made for brighter days

SANJHH BY AM is a contemporary women's fashion label built around one belief — good clothes make brighter days. We design for the woman who dresses for herself: silhouettes that move with her, colours that lift her mood, and details that feel personal rather than loud.

From breezy jumpsuits and tailored co-ords to occasion-ready shararas, every piece is created to be worn again and again — timeless rather than trend-led, elegant rather than excessive.
