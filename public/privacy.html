Privacy Policy

What we collect

When you place an order we collect the details needed to fulfil it: your name, mobile number, email, and delivery address. Your wishlist, bag and saved checkout details live in your own browser's storage.

How we use it

  • To process, produce, ship and support your orders.
  • To respond to messages you send via the contact page.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Payments

Online payments are processed by a secure third-party payment gateway. Your card/UPI credentials are handled by the gateway and never touch our servers; we only receive a payment confirmation reference.

Contact

For any privacy request (access, correction, deletion), reach us through the contact page.
