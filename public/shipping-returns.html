Good To Know

Shipping & Returns

Made to order, with care

Many SANJHH BY AM pieces are prepared after you order — this keeps quality high and waste low. You can follow every step (production, quality check, packing, dispatch) live on the Track Order page.

Shipping

  • Free shipping on qualifying orders (the current threshold is shown in your bag and at checkout).
  • Orders below the threshold ship at a flat fee shown at checkout.
  • Once dispatched, your courier name and AWB/tracking number appear in Track Order.
  • Delivery timelines vary by PIN code and courier serviceability.

Cash on Delivery

COD is available on serviceable PIN codes. A small COD handling charge, if applicable, is shown clearly at checkout before you place the order.

Exchanges

  • Size exchanges are supported. Raise a request within 48 hours of delivery via the contact page with your order ID.
  • Items must be unused, unwashed, with tags intact.
  • Embroidered occasion wear may have limited exchange windows — mentioned on the product page.

Returns & refunds

  • If a piece arrives damaged or incorrect, contact us within 48 hours with photos and your order ID — we'll make it right.
  • Approved refunds for prepaid orders are processed to the original payment method.

This policy page is editable from the store settings — final published policy should be reviewed by the brand before launch.
