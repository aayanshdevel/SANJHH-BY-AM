Our Story

Made For Brighter Days

Good clothes, brighter days.

SANJHH BY AM began with a simple idea: the right outfit can quietly change your whole day. We make contemporary womenswear that celebrates individuality and femininity — clothes you reach for again and again, because they feel like you.

Our pieces favour timeless silhouettes over passing trends: breezy jumpsuits, tailored co-ords, romantic dresses and occasion wear inspired by tradition and finished with modern ease.

What We Believe

Details make it timeless

A hand-tied bow. A smocked back that actually fits. A daisy-trimmed hem that makes you smile. We obsess over the small things so that every piece feels personal, wearable and worth keeping.

Fashion for a kinder tomorrow — designed thoughtfully, made to be loved for longer.
